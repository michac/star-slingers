Layout mock, not playable. Tap a fleet ship to hit it: the first hit pops its shield, the second loses the ship. Asteroids in a lost ship's sublane fly past harmlessly. Between waves rebuilds lost ships and restores every shield. Show aim reach draws each seat's aim range (15° to 165°). Show boss draws the command-ship boss where it parks today, with its beam on the top-most live ship. Rockets fly over the fleet; only enemies hit it.
