Controls v2 — seat options

Each link opens the game with those seats. The choice is saved on this phone, so the home-screen app keeps it until you pick another. Aim: slide a thumb sideways on your right to turn the aim. FIRE: hold to charge, release to shoot. HOME: hold to see the lock, release for a homing shot. Left-handed flips a seat: aim on the left, buttons on the right.
