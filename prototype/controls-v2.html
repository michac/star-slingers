Controls v2

Who plays (one seat or both) and Easy or Normal is picked on the join screen the game opens on. Aim: slide a thumb sideways on your right to turn the aim. FIRE: hold to charge, release to shoot. SPECIAL: hold to see the target, release for a homing missile. Left-handed: hold the small ⇄ above your FIRE button until its ring fills, and your seat flips (aim on the left, buttons on the right). Hold it again to flip back. The phone remembers each seat's choice.
